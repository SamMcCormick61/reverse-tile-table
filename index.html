The game's over. Now find the letters.

A full game has already been played to the last tile, then every tile was turned face down. You draw seven letters from the same bag and put each one back where you think it was played.

Players

Play solo, or pass one device around for up to four players.

Play online

Up to four players, each on their own phone or computer. There are no turns: everyone races to place tiles at the same time. The host's board and helper settings are used.

Board

Words on the board

A brand-new finished game is played out for you.

Clocks

Turn timer (each player, every turn)
Game clock (whole game)

Run out of turn time and your tiles go back on your rack and play moves on. When the game clock hits zero the game ends and unplayed tiles count against you.
