Privacy Policy

Reverse Tile Table · Last updated 28 October 2026

Reverse Tile Table is a word tile game made by Sam McCormick. This policy covers the Android app and the web version at sammccormick61.github.io/reverse-tile-table.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it sends nothing about you or how you play to us or anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your current game, your settings and the player names you type in are saved on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. They never leave your device. Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling it, deletes them.

Internet use

  • The Android app works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.
  • The web version loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when the page opens. Google's own privacy policy covers that.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds something that handles your data, such as in-app purchases or ads, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the game's GitHub issues page.
